CivSim

A world history machine. Nobody plays it: pick a seed, press play, and watch history write itself. (Phase 16: after the factories come electricity, engines, radio and aircraft, then the atom. Alliance blocs fight world wars and found leagues after them; a few great realms build nuclear weapons, and crises between them usually pass. When one doesn't, cities burn and a nuclear winter follows, and laboratories or a warming climate can bring catastrophe too. Try the Alliance blocs and Nuclear powers map modes and the nuclear weapons chart. Every world still ends: in conquest, cultural dominance, religious unity, world peace, self-annihilation, extinction, or the time limit; watch the race to the end and read the epilogue.)
